Exact validation of majority-block Walsh coefficients. Markers show complete truth-table enumeration followed by a normalized fast Walsh-Hadamard transform; solid lines show the analytical coefficient laws and the dashed gray curve is the exponential reference 2^{-b/2}. (a) For odd blocks, the singleton and weight-three sectors follow c_{b,1}=eta_b and c_{b,3}=-eta_b/(b-2), while all even-weight coefficients vanish. (b) For even blocks with deterministic ties mapped to majority bit 0, d_{b,0}=d_{b,1} and d_{b,2}=d_{b,3}=-eta_b/(b-1). Across all 2,097,148 enumerated coefficients, the maximum complete-spectrum discrepancy was 1.08e-19.
